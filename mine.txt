Introduction
1.1
Coupled magnetic mechanical oscillator
Simple harmonic motion refers to the motion a body undergoes when the restoring force is directly propor-
tional to the displacement from equilibrium.
A simple harmonic oscillator is a system that oscillates with simple harmonic motion. For example, a mass
attached to an elastic spring that is freely subjected to gravity is a simple harmonic oscillator.
A coupled harmonic oscillator consists of two or more simple harmonic oscillators that are connected to-
gether, such that energy can be transferred between them.
In this extended essay, the coupled magnetic mechanical oscillator consists of two identical copper leaf
springs that are clamped vertically at their lower ends to a rigid and non-magnetic base, as shown in Figure 1.
A permanent neodymium magnet is attached to the free end of each spring. The two magnets are oriented so
that they repel, hence the like poles face each other.
