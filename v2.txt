2. Leakage & windows. Rectangular: narrowest main lobe (best for two close peaks of similar

synthetic test (T = 20 s) the errors were ±2–7 mHz at 21–31 mm, ±10 mHz at 35 mm and total
failure at 40 mm — quote your own numbers from ee_simulations.py Part A with your real T
